No original ROMs, executable game files, sprite sheets, title logos, characters, level maps, manuals, music or audio samples are included. Links to historical descriptions are references, not game dependencies or asset sources.

The historical titles Dogs of War, Cops 'n' Robbers and Ninja Combat identify the maintainer's inspirations. Their rights remain with the respective holders; this project does not imply their affiliation or endorsement. The first game identities are Iron Meridian, Velvet Heist and Neon Ronin. Collection 002 adds Neon Air, Basement Brawl, Chrono Wing, Prism Break and Void Command.

Contributors must document provenance before introducing external material and preserve applicable notices. Do not treat an abandonware listing as a licence to redistribute original assets.

Collection 002 references air hockey, Pit-Fighter (1990), Time Pilot (1982), Breakout (1976) and Star Trek (1971) as gameplay inspirations. Void Command uses no franchise ships, factions, characters, vocabulary, symbols or story: the independent courier Lantern, Free Relay Service and Cinder Array are original to this project. Its terminal interface was designed from the maintainer's requested visual direction; the supplied historical screenshot is not redistributed as a game asset.

All new geometry, fighter silhouettes, aircraft, brick layouts, terminal panels, sound arrangements and game code were authored for this repository. Neon Air's projected geometry is software-generated; no 3D models or textures were imported. `collection-002-v030.png` and `void-command-v030.png` are documentation renders of the new game's Canvas code, not historical assets or browser screenshots.
