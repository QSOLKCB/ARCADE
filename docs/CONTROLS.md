# How to play

Choose Arcade or Relaxed difficulty and one or two players before opening a cabinet. The action games support two local players. Velvet Heist is single-player. Sound, optional CRT scanlines and touch controls can be toggled; fullscreen keeps the cabinet controls available. Escape or P pauses. Losing window focus pauses automatically; use Resume to continue.

## Iron Meridian

Move with WASD (or arrows when solo). Hold J or Z to shoot in your facing direction. Moving the mouse over the game enables mouse aim; left mouse fires and right mouse throws a grenade. Keyboard-only play never needs a mouse. K or X throws a limited grenade; Space or C performs a short invulnerable dash. E cycles purchased weapons. At zero ammunition, the weapon automatically falls back to the unlimited sidearm.

Buy a team loadout before deployment. Equipment persists across the three contracts; ammo, health and grenades refill between contracts. Purchases cannot overdraw credits. Armour increases maximum health. Each contract awards credits for the next loadout.

Follow the road north, collect all three marked objectives by approaching them, destroy the armoured roadblock, and step onto the extraction pad at the north end. Health and ammo packs are scattered along the route. Buildings block movement and bullets. Grenades and rockets deal blast damage. There is no friendly fire.

In co-op, keep together so both players remain within the shared camera. Hold E (player 1) or H (player 2) beside a fallen partner for a revive. A contract ends in failure only when everyone is down. After the third extraction, the campaign ends in victory.

## Velvet Heist

Move through the maze with WASD or arrows. Diagonal input chooses a cardinal direction. E uses nearby marked doors. The courtyard connects to the exchange, mine and lockup; the exchange leads upstairs to the vault. You can return through every door.

Pick up 18 loose diamonds and the code scraps in the exchange, mine and lockup. Hold E for 1.5 seconds beside the upper-vault safe after collecting all three scraps; it contains four more gems. Return all 22 gems to the car and press E to bank them and complete the heist. Banking is allowed in multiple trips.

J or Z fires one of eight stun rounds in your last movement direction. K or X creates a temporary smoke cloud. Police follow walkable corridors and are stunned by bullets. Mine ghosts move through walls and ignore bullets and smoke: keep moving around them. There is no jump action in this cabinet.

The car refills rounds and smoke. Getting caught costs one life, drops the bag at the capture point, and returns you to the car; already banked gems and code scraps remain yours. Dropped gems can be collected again. Visit the lockup holding cell and press E to restore a captured life. Lose the final life and the run ends.

## Neon Ronin

Move left and right along the street, or up and down between lanes. J or Z strikes enemies nearby and throws stars at enemies farther away. Face your target. Space or C jumps over attacks and strengthens close-range attacks while airborne. K or X triggers a storm for 60 energy. Energy recharges over time and on enemy defeat.

Pick up food for health, staffs for longer and stronger melee reach, and ember stars for stronger projectiles. Defeat every enemy in a block, then follow GO to the right. The fourth block in each district contains a boss; enemies show a warning before attacking. Three districts complete the campaign. Health refills between districts.

In co-op, hold E or H beside a fallen partner to revive them. Stay together; both players use the same camera.

## Other inputs

Player 2: arrows move; U attacks; I uses special; O jumps/dashes; H uses or revives. Numpad 1, 2, 0 and 3 are alternatives. Standard controllers use left stick/D-pad for movement, A/B/X/Y for attack/special/jump/use, and Start to pause/resume. Up to two standard-mapped controllers are read; non-standard devices may need keyboard input.

Touch controls use pointer capture and support holding a direction while attacking. Use two directional buttons for diagonal movement in the action games. Cancelled touches release their actions. The launcher and menus support keyboard focus. Canvas gameplay itself is visual and is not screen-reader equivalent.
